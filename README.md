## What is Zassou?
Zassou (雑草) is mostly my first attempt and trying to learn how to make an Anki addon
It is also my first coding project in a long time, so I am trying to derust by overengineering something super simple LOL

## What Does Zassou Do?
Zassou finds the cards you have trouble with the most in your specified Anki decks, and presents them to you on your home page so you can give them special attention
I had this idea because I kept getting "super" leeches, which were leeches I would suspend, delete, remine, and then end up suspending again
